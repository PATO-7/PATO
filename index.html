    The Atelier

    In a candlelit workshop where dust drifts through single beams of light, PATO assembles time by hand. No conveyors. No hurry. Each movement rests on 212 components, bevelled until they mirror the flame.

    We release only three series a year: Obsidian for the midnight gallery, Ember for those who keep candle hours, and Lunar for patient watchers of the sky. Every case is cold-forged, every dial lacquered in eleven passes, every second hand balanced like a whispered promise.

    “A watch should wind the wearer’s day, not the other way around.”— Head of Atelier
